# Song Finder

**Live site** https://evanbeitler.github.io/ai101midterm/

## Audience and purpose
For people who love music and are getting bored of the same playlist, Song Finder lets them type a few songs or artists and get new songs from the same genre, with a link to listen.

## The two features
1. **Genre detection and recommendations (interactive):** type songs or artists separated by commas, press Find songs, and see the detected genre plus up to 8 other songs from it. Choose Spotify, YouTube Music or Apple Music for the Listen links.
2. **Browse panels:** a Top 5 genres list (click one to see its songs) and a Popular songs list of 10 with Listen links.

## How to use
1. Type songs or artists, for example `Blinding Lights, Kendrick Lamar`.
2. Pick your streaming platform, then press Enter or Find songs.
3. Or skip typing and click a genre on the left, or a Listen link on the right.

## Known limitations
- Only the 40 songs in the sample data are recognized; anything else shows a "not in the sample data" message.
- No real AI runs in the page yet, and no saved lists between visits.
- Recommendations are the other songs in the same genre, not personalized.
- Listen links open a search on the platform, not a guaranteed exact match.
